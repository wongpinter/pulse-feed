<p><i>Pulse Feed</i> is a fast, privacy-focused feed reader for RSS 2.0, Atom and JSON Feed. It keeps your subscriptions and read state on your own device: no account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads.</p><p><b><u>Highlights</u></b></p><ul><li>Subscribe from a URL pasted into the app or shared from any browser</li><li>Automatic feed discovery for plain website addresses</li><li>Folders to group sources, plus per-feed mute</li><li>OPML import and export for moving in or out</li><li>Offline-first reading: articles are stored locally, so lists and opened articles work without a connection</li><li>Full-text search across everything you have stored, not just the title</li><li>Read later list with per-article read and bookmark state</li><li>Background refresh with optional new-article notifications and quiet hours</li><li>Clean reader view plus a hardened in-app browser, with ad-host blocking on Android</li><li>Material You theming, dark and AMOLED dark themes, and adjustable typography</li><li>Optional on-device AI summaries on supported devices</li></ul><p><i>Pulse Feed</i> is an open-source fork of <i>Harmonic</i> by Simon Halvdansson and is licensed under Apache 2.0. It is not affiliated with any news service or publisher whose feeds you choose to add.</p>
